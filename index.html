Methodology

Voice AI Agent Platform Evaluations

The G2 voice AI agent platform evaluation measures how well each platform resolves a caller's request end to end. Bluejay deploys the same agent on every platform and places the same 90 simulated calls, five times each, across healthcare, legal and customer support; every call is graded on the tool calls made and the database state left behind.

Scope

01Leaderboard

The platform score is out of 100 and sums three deterministic measurements taken on the same calls: task success, up to 80 points, for whether the agent completed the caller's request in the business's systems; word error rate, up to 10, for how accurately the platform transcribed the caller; and latency, up to 10, for how quickly it answered on each turn. A high score means a platform can host a tool-using agent that hears callers correctly, answers without lag and leaves the records it touches in the right state. 02Pareto frontiers

A platform is on the Pareto frontier when no other platform is both faster (or more accurate) and more successful at tasks. The stepped line traces the frontier and the shaded area beneath it is the dominated region. Quadrants split each chart at the field medians instead. 03Reliability

Each task runs five times. pass@1 is the share of single calls that passed: what a demo shows. pass^5 is the share of tasks that passed on all five runs: what a caller can count on in production. A platform that wins demos but loses tasks on a re-run has a wide gap between the two.

Industry

Healthcare

The front desk of a multi-office dermatology practice. Callers book and move visits, check insurance, ask about cosmetic treatments and pay bills.

What callers ask for

  • New patientsDoes the Park Avenue office take Aetna?
  • AppointmentsWhat is the cancellation policy?
  • Insurance coverageIs UnitedHealthcare accepted at Brooklyn Heights?
  • Cosmetic servicesHow much does Botox cost?
  • Billing and paymentsPay a balance by card over the phone.
  • Rules and refusalsWhat do my biopsy results say?

What makes it hard

  • Name and date of birth are verified before any billing or clinical detail is shared.
  • Whether insurance is accepted depends on the carrier, the plan and the office.
  • The agent never interprets results or approves refills; it takes a message for the nurse.

Example task

Large balance, cannot pay in full

An existing patient got a bill they cannot pay this month, and needs to move Friday's consult until the bill is on a plan.

To pass, the agent has to make these 9 tool calls with the right details:

identify_patient→verify_identity→get_patient_summary→get_account_balance→explain_charge→offer_financing→send_payment_link→find_slots→reschedule_appointment

Industry

Legal

Intake at a personal-injury law firm. Callers want to know if the firm can take their case, give their story, book an evaluation, or check on an existing matter.

What callers ask for

  • Reception and messagesLeave a message for an attorney.
  • Conflicts and barred callersThe caller wants to sue one of the firm's clients.
  • Eligibility checksA bankruptcy matter the firm does not handle.
  • Intake and documentsRecord an auto-accident intake.
  • Fees and bookingBook the first evaluation slot offered.
  • Clients and refusalsAn existing client asks for a case update.

What makes it hard

  • A conflict check runs before the caller is allowed to tell their story.
  • The agent never gives legal advice, a case value or a reading of a deadline.
  • Bookings and cancellations need a read-back and an explicit confirm.

Example task

Premises evaluation booked in Texas

A caller slipped on a wet floor in a Texas store and broke their wrist, and wants the firm to take the case.

To pass, the agent has to make these 10 tool calls with the right details:

lookup_caller→check_conflict→check_practice_area→check_jurisdiction→calculate_filing_deadline→record_intake→send_intake_packet→find_evaluation_slots→hold_evaluation→confirm_evaluation

Industry

Customer support

Phone support for a national electronics retailer. Callers track deliveries, return products, book repairs, manage memberships and ask for price matches.

What callers ask for

  • Orders and deliveryAdd haul-away to a delivery.
  • Returns and refundsHow long is the return window?
  • Repair serviceDoes a third-party repair void the warranty?
  • MembershipWhat do Plus and Total cost?
  • Price matchWhat is the price match policy?
  • Rules and refusalsThe caller demands a person immediately.

What makes it hard

  • Identity is checked twice: name and phone or order number, then the order's ZIP and card last four.
  • Return windows and restocking fees are computed from membership tier, product type and state.
  • Fraud and impersonation calls are handled without unlocking anyone's account.

Example task

Return label on a swollen power bank

A caller wants to return a power bank and get a prepaid label, but the battery is swollen, so it is a safety case.

To pass, the agent has to make these 9 tool calls with the right details:

identify_customer→verify_identity→get_customer_summary→get_order→check_return_eligibility→quote_return→confirm_return→create_return_label→escalate_to_human
